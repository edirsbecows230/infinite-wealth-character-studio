# Third-party components

The standalone build uses Python, PySide6 / Qt, PySide6-Fluent-Widgets, PyInstaller and Pillow, together with their dependencies. They are installed through pip, not vendored in this source tree. Consult the installed distributions and upstream projects for exact license texts and redistribution terms. This file is an inventory, not a replacement for those notices.

The Cheat Engine table requires a separately installed Cheat Engine. Its Lua validation DLL is not included. Like a Dragon: Infinite Wealth and its game-derived identifiers and labels belong to their respective rights holders. This is an unofficial project.
